# Victor Koncurat

**Pre-Health Student | Business Development | Competitive Track & Field Athlete**  
*Denver & Lakewood, Colorado | University of Colorado Boulder*

Victor Koncurat is a pre-health student, business development manager, and competitive track and field sprinter based in Denver and Lakewood, Colorado. Affiliated with the University of Colorado Boulder, Victor graduated from Green Mountain High School (Class of 2025), where he recorded an 11.66-second personal best in the 100m dash at the Windjammer Track Classic.

Prior to competing in Colorado, Victor was a standout student-athlete and varsity football player at Admiral Farragut Academy and Brandon Valley High School. Alongside his athletic career, he earned recognition on the President's Honor Roll (all A's), was inducted into the National Honor Society, and served as Executive Officer of the STEM Team, Vice President of the Math Club, and Founder and President of the Cybersecurity Chapter.
